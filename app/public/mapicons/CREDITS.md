# Map icon credits

The Fantasy Map Builder's built-in icon library uses artwork from
**[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net)**, bundled via the `react-icons`
"Game Icons" (`gi`) set.

These icons are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)**:

Authors include Lorc, Delapouite and contributors. Full per-icon attribution is
available at <https://game-icons.net/about.html#authors>.

User-imported icons (the "My Imports" tab) are the property of their respective
creators and are stored only inside your own project.
